# Chapter 20 — Golden Matter: Particle Masses and Mixing

## Overview

This chapter explores the golden ratio φ in particle masses and mixing matrices.

## 20.1 Lepton Masses

### 20.1.1 Tau-Lepton Mass Ratio

$$\frac{m_τ}{m_μ} ≈ \frac{1776.86}{105.66} ≈ 16.82$$

Golden ratio approximations:

$$\frac{m_τ}{m_μ} ≈ π^2 φ ≈ 16.0$$

The π²φ approximation is within 5%.

## 20.2 Gauge Boson Masses

### 20.2.1 W and Z Boson Masses

$$m_W ≈ 80.379 \text{ GeV}$$
$$m_Z ≈ 91.1876 \text{ GeV}$$

Ratio:

$$\frac{m_Z}{m_W} ≈ 1.133$$

Golden ratio expression:

$$φ^{1/4} ≈ 1.127$$ (within 0.5%)

### 20.2.2 Higgs Boson Mass

$$m_H ≈ 125.10 \text{ GeV}$$

Ratio to W mass:

$$\frac{m_H}{m_W} ≈ 1.556$$

Golden ratio expression:

$$\frac{π}{2} ≈ 1.571$$ (within 1%)

## 20.3 CKM Matrix

### 20.3.1 Wolfenstein Parameters

- λ ≈ 0.22653
- A ≈ 0.790

Golden ratio relations:

$$A ≈ \frac{φ}{2} ≈ 0.809$$ (within 2.4%)

## 20.4 Summary

Particle masses and mixing show golden ratio patterns:

1. m_τ/m_μ ≈ π²φ with 5% accuracy
2. m_Z/m_W ≈ φ^(1/4) with 0.5% accuracy
3. m_H/m_W ≈ π/2 with 1% accuracy
4. CKM parameter A ≈ φ/2 with 2.4% accuracy
