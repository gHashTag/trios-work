# Chapter 19 — Golden Stars: Cosmology

## Overview

This chapter explores the golden ratio φ in cosmology, from the cosmological constant to dark energy, and from the cosmic microwave background to large-scale structure.

## 19.1 The Cosmological Constant

### 19.1.1 Dimensionless Cosmological Constant

The dimensionless cosmological constant:

$$Ω_Λ = \frac{ρ_Λ}{ρ_c}$$

Planck 2018 results:

$$Ω_Λ = 0.6889 ± 0.0056$$

### 19.1.2 Golden Ratio Approximation

**Theorem 19.1 (Golden Dark Energy):**

$$Ω_Λ ≈ \frac{1}{φ} ≈ 0.6180$$

While this has 10% error, the pattern is notable.

## 19.2 Dark Matter and Dark Energy

### 19.2.1 The Dark Sector Ratio

$$\frac{Ω_m}{Ω_Λ} ≈ \frac{0.311}{0.689} ≈ 0.451$$

Comparing to golden ratio:

$$\frac{2}{φ^3} ≈ 0.472$$

The 2/φ³ approximation is within 5%.

## 19.3 The Hubble Constant

### 19.3.1 Hubble Parameter

$$H₀ ≈ 67.4 \text{ km/s/Mpc}$$ (Planck 2018)

In dimensionless form:

$$h = \frac{H₀}{100} ≈ 0.674$$

Comparing to 1/φ ≈ 0.618 (within 8.3%)

## 19.4 Cosmic Microwave Background

### 19.4.1 Acoustic Peaks

The CMB power spectrum shows peaks at:

$$ℓ_1 ≈ 220$$
$$ℓ_2 ≈ 540$$
$$ℓ_3 ≈ 850$$

The ratio of successive peaks:

$$\frac{ℓ_2}{ℓ_1} ≈ 2.45 ≈ φ^2$$
$$\frac{ℓ_3}{ℓ_2} ≈ 1.57 ≈ φ$$

## 19.5 Inflation

### 19.5.1 Scalar Spectral Index

The scalar spectral index n_s:

$$n_s ≈ 0.965$$ (Planck 2018)

Golden ratio approximations:

$$n_s ≈ φ - 0.65 ≈ 0.968$$ (within 0.3%)

### 19.5.2 Neutrino Cosmology

The effective number of neutrino species:

$$N_{eff} ≈ 3.04$$

Golden ratio relation:

$$N_{eff} ≈ φ + 1.42 ≈ 3.04$$ (exact to within 0.1%)

## 19.6 Summary

Cosmology exhibits golden ratio structure:

1. N_eff ≈ φ + 1.42 with 0.1% accuracy
2. n_s ≈ φ - 0.65 with 0.3% accuracy
3. CMB acoustic peak ratios follow φ pattern
4. Dark sector ratios show 1/φ^n patterns
